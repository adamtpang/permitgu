# Government of Guam — Comprehensive Agency List

**Compiled:** Nov 9, 2026 (America/Chicago)  
**Purpose:** Support Permit GU product planning. Counts depend on how entities are classified; this file documents the major official lists and reconciles Adam's "~51 agencies" figure.

---

## What "~51 agencies" refers to

Speaker Frank Blas Jr. said at a Guam Chamber of Commerce Republican Gubernatorial Forum (Jun 25, 2026) that while the U.S. federal government serves hundreds of millions with about 12 cabinet departments, **"Guam has 51 agencies for 150,000 people."**  
Source: [Pacific Daily News coverage](https://www.guampdn.com/news/republican-gubernatorial-teams-talk-economy-governance-and-modernization/article_f7db2a60-2f2b-4f3a-9b74-05dbdc87bd0b.html).

That **51** is a political/colloquial round number, not a single statutory inventory. Closest official inventories:

| Source | Count | Notes |
|--------|------:|-------|
| DOA/HR **2023 Department/Agency Listing** (GHI RFP attachment) | **58 numbered rows** | Non-autonomous 1–40 + autonomous 41–58; GGRF split into two rows (retiree vs staff) → **~57 unique** if merged | [PDF](https://hr.doa.guam.gov/wp-hr-content/uploads/2023/05/FY2024-GHI-RFP-Amendment-III-Attachment-Department-Listing.pdf) |
| Guam Legislature **GovGuam Web Directory** | **~70 listings** | Broader: adds boards (PEALS, Accountancy, CLB), Judiciary, Congressman Moylan, sub-units | [Directory](https://guamlegislature.gov/govguam-web-directory/) |
| DOA **Official Portal Directory** | Overlaps Legislature list | Missing some executive offices present on Legislature page | [doa.guam.gov/official-portal-directory](https://doa.guam.gov/official-portal-directory/) |
| 12 GCA Title 12 (Autonomous Agencies) | Statutory chapters | Airport, KGTF, GHC, GHURA, GRTA, GPA, GVB, Port, PDSC, PUC, GWA, etc. | [Justia Title 12 Div. 1](https://law.justia.com/codes/guam/2024/title-12/division-1/) |

**Recommended working total for Permit GU planning:** treat **~57–58 DOA-listed entities** as the core executive/agency universe, and treat **boards & commissions** (PEALS, CLB, Accountancy, ABC Board, health boards, GLUC, etc.) as an **additional permit-issuing layer** (~15–25 bodies) that issue licenses even when not separately numbered on the DOA GHI list. A full "citizen can touch this entity" universe is closer to **70+**.

## Recommended totals (how to cite them)

1. **Colloquial "~51":** Blas quote, Jun 2026 Chamber forum ([PDN](https://www.guampdn.com/news/republican-gubernatorial-teams-talk-economy-governance-and-modernization/article_f7db2a60-2f2b-4f3a-9b74-05dbdc87bd0b.html)).  
2. **Official DOA GHI inventory:** **58 numbered / ~57 unique** agencies ([PDF](https://hr.doa.guam.gov/wp-hr-content/uploads/2023/05/FY2024-GHI-RFP-Amendment-III-Attachment-Department-Listing.pdf)).  
3. **Directory breadth (Legislature):** **~70** named entries including boards and federal delegate ([directory](https://guamlegislature.gov/govguam-web-directory/)).  
4. **Permit GU product framing:** "~51–60 core agencies + boards/commissions" is accurate enough; do not invent a precise single integer beyond citing the source.

# Guam Permits, Licenses & Approvals Catalog

**Compiled:** Nov 9, 2026 (America/Chicago)  
**Purpose:** Inventory of citizen/business-facing GovGuam applications for Permit GU planning.  
**Method:** Built from official agency forms pages, BLPC/BSP resources, and statute references. **Not exhaustive.** Items without a live official URL this turn are marked **unverified**. Federal permits (USACE, USFWS, etc.) are noted only where they gate local clearances.

**MVP for Permit GU:** residential one-family building permit (DPW) — see `/workspace/permit-gu/spec.md`.

---

## How to read this catalog

| Column | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| Item | Permit / license / clearance / certificate |
| Agency | Issuing or lead agency |
| Source | Official URL or primary document |

Counts at the bottom are **catalog entries**, not unique statutes.
